Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image: a newspaper front page with 37 date-checked
stories across 9 regions and 24 fields — three illustrated leads (COP31's deal
names no fossil fuels; Russia fires 186 drones over Ukraine; Iran's radar claim
meets Bahrain's denial), a numbers strip, region coverage bars, a UTC strip, and
a full numbered story index. Every story is tagged by source and confidence
(confirmed / reported / claimed).

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`; both runs
write identical bytes, no network.
